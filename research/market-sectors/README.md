# BRAINK-AGENT public research draft

This screened research-only edition contains 93 candidate technology-market problems, 186 proposed delivery/analyst team identities, 94 distinct public primary-source URLs, a proposed canonical-taxonomy crosswalk and the independent-process review.

The three registers contain 43 compute/service, 27 AI/data/security/HCI/verification and 23 edge/hardware/circularity/adoption candidates. Each retains its buyer hypothesis, observed problem, source evidence and limitations, alternatives, proposed intervention, team specialties, acceptance criteria and dependencies. None establishes customer demand, instantiated teams, qualified expertise or solved bottlenecks.

## Files and use

- Read the three market-bottlenecks JSON files or complete TXT companions.
- Use market-sector-review-crosswalk.json for all 93 proposed row mappings. It retains the owner's 29 technical sector IDs, 18 independent foundry names and 18 application-sector names as separate axes. Functions and ambiguous/application-gap mappings remain unresolved.
- Read market-sector-independent-review.json before implementation. Review findings and their correction states remain visible.
- Resolve public evidence through public-source-index.json. Source namespaces are compute, ai_data and edge_adoption; technical_sector is a separate namespace. Never join bare S01 tokens across types.
- MANIFEST.json contains byte lengths and SHA-256 hashes for this exact edition.
