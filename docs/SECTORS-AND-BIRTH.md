# Sector teams, native birth and expertise

## Birth contract

1. Resolve immutable purpose and admitted source. Preserve BRAINK root and original product identities.
2. Derive a caller-scoped family undertaking through the native workflow. Register scoped capability/task identity, continuation, memory relations, workbook preferences and source-backed claim in the native transaction.
3. Instantiate native team and member identities from the immutable template closure. Do not relabel a UUID as a hosted principal or a ChatGPT conversation.
4. Retain the birth packet in native VFS, fetch actual bytes and verify the receipt chain.
5. Keep expertise unqualified until an appropriate executable binding and applied evidence exist.

Native local team members have manager, administration, production, specialist and verification responsibilities. Their immutable identities and storage scopes remain distinct. These scopes are trusted-code API boundaries, not OS sandbox or credential isolation.

## Sector admission

`admit_sector` requires a stable ID, title, bottleneck, source URL/title/claim/access date, delivery purpose, analyst purpose and foundry references. The source record is immutable. Its delivery and analyst teams are born separately; their member VFS roots do not overlap. Records are owner-only by default.

Technical sectors S01–S29, application sectors, functions, 18 independent foundries and market bottlenecks are separate axes. This release preserves supplied references without inventing the missing full register. `foundryMapping=UNADMITTED_REFERENCES` explicitly prevents a reference string from becoming a grant or a qualified mapping.

## Qualification contract

A sector's desired expertise must resolve to an exact typed capability, admitted source and executable binding. A qualifying record needs the source version, model/runtime identity when applicable, benchmark ID/version, input/output digests, actual actor run, durable artifact readback, analyst identity and findings, scope, exclusions and separate assessment receipt when required.

The delivery team cannot establish independent assessment by renaming its own output. An analyst team must perform its own scoped work and return findings. A different process hashing bytes is deterministic verification; it is not a different-model review. Model bindings are null here. No sector domain expertise, market solution or all-estate qualification is claimed by template instantiation.

## Memory and learning

Authoritative memory is retained native history and immutable objects. A future retrieval index may be pruned or rebuilt without being treated as the sole historical record. This release claims tested restart recovery, not infinite retention or that memory can never be lost.

Learning follows propose → review → compare → validate → commit/reject as a documented workflow. Merely storing that preference does not execute scientific validation. New claims need evidence, version binding and reviewer findings before promotion.

Research artifacts are external supplied inputs. Until their bytes and rights are inspected, the repository keeps an empty research admission surface rather than invented bottleneck records.
